# 2026-11-01: gumline inflammation

NOTE: I did not synchronously record the date, so it may be off by one
or two days. I noted the effects in a Slack self-chat logs message on
2026-11-02, which was after the initial day of symptoms, but I'm not
sure if it was one or two days after, so the actual date might be
2026-10-31 or 2026-11-01.

Some time during or after my first meal, I noticed a sensation of
irritation in the gumline inferior to the interdental gap between my
facial mandibular central incisors (so right in the middle between
left and right). This felt like something, like a food particle or
irritant, had gotten down there under the gumline and was causing
itching. However, whatever it was was not accessible to flossing.

Palpating the area identified the point just a few millimeters below
the interdental gap as the most sensitive area. The sensitivity seemed
to be from underneath rather than on the surface of the gumline.

It was not distracting except when deliberately palpated or stretched.

It got better gradually over the next few days. By 2026-11-04, it had
effectively healed.
