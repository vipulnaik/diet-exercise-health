# Standing on heels with eyes closed (with toe support) (with history)

## Goal and functional relevance of exercise

This exercise is the eyes-closed version of [standing on heels with
eyes open with toe
support](standing-on-heels-with-eyes-open-with-toe-support-with-history.md)
and has a similar expected benefit of improving the strength and
endurance of the muscles involved in heel standing. The transition of
the cycle exercise from eyes-open to eyes-closed was for time
efficiency: my duration for eyes-open was long enough, so increasing
the complexity of the exercise to eyes-closed was a way to continue to
keep it time-efficient.

## Protocol

I stand on heels with my toes supported by a sled base runner, usually
with the legs at an angle pointing toward the middle (for reasons of
geometry rather than a deliberate choice). I try not to rest on the
toe support, putting most of my weight on the heels and using the toe
support only to maintain balance and stability. I keep my eyes closed. I
stay in this position for as long as possible.

other balance exercises. This is partly because the bottleneck isn't
