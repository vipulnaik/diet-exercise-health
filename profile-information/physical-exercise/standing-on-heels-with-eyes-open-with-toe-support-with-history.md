# Standing on heels with eyes open (with toe support) (with history)

## Goal and functional relevance of exercise

This exercise is a slight variation of [standing on heels with eyes
open](standing-on-heels-with-eyes-open-with-history.md). The original
exercise suffered from the limitation that the stability of standing
on heels is highly sensitive to initial configuration, and the stable
basin of configurations is small, so minor perturbations lead to
losing balance. Therefore, it does not provide the strength/endurance
training benefit that only comes from longer durations.

With toe support (toes lightly resting on a raised surface,
specifically, a flat surface like a sled base runner of a table, the
stability is taken care of, since any slight loss of balance is
absorbed by the toe support as I fall forward. Therefore, I can focus
on duration.

## Protocol

I stand on heels with my toes supported by a sled base runner, usually
with the legs at an angle pointing toward the middle (for reasons of
geometry rather than a deliberate choice). I try not to rest on the
toe support, putting most of my weight on the heels and using the toe
support only to maintain balance and stability. I keep my eyes open. I
stay in this position for as long as possible.

## History

### History of durations

I don't set any specific threshold for the exercise the way I do for
other balance exercises. This is partly because it's an eyes-open
exercise and partly because the bottleneck isn't the sort where
willpower and effort to push through play a big role. Here is a rough
picture of how the durations have evolved.

Date | Rough estimate of average duration across recent tries (in seconds)
-- | --
2026-03-24 (first attempt) | 77 (score on the first try)
2026-06-17 | 140 to 170
2026-09-19 (last attempt as cycle exercise) | 180 to 210

### History of triggers for overall exercise

#### Settings 2026-03-24 to 2026-09-19

I added this exercise to the cycle exercises in the [balance exercise
cycle](balance-exercise-cycle-with-history.md) on 2026-03-24, and did
my last execution of it as a cycle exercise on 2026-09-19.

#### Settings 2026-09-19 onward

Starting 2026-09-19, I introduced the corresponding eyes-closed
exercise ([standing on heels with eyes closed (with toe
support)](standing-on-heels-with-eyes-closed-with-toe-support-with-history.md)). The
eyes-closed version became the cycle exercise, and the eyes-open
version became an occasional exercise swapped in for it.

The reason for the change was that my eyes-open duration had become
long enough that it was no longer a time-efficient exercise; the
eyes-closed exercise, with the additional effort it needed, was the
right fit at my enhanced performance level.
